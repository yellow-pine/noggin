MuJoCo and drawn by Blender in CI on every merge to main, never kept in git (ADR 0009, ADR 0019). How the pictures are
made: [Model notes](model-notes.md).

First each Stage in the viewer, turning as you drag it, then its stills. Stage by Stage, in the Preset the Theme bar
at the top picks, Cansin's build (its Skeleton mechanical, all black) by
default, or Morty or Bart (their Skeleton as Bones): Stage 1 the eye
block on its test stand with the eyes Core, Stage 2 adds the Torso, the neck and the boards, Stage 3 the jaw, the whole
Mech. Under them, as the home shows them,
Stage 1 from the front and Stage 3 from the side, exploded. Then each Stage with every part apart, each slid out the
way it goes on: a servo or horn along its shaft, a part on a pivot along its axis, a board off its carrier. Turn each in
[the viewer](https://yellowpine.com/noggin/viewer/).

Stage 1 in the viewer, once per motion, each playing it over and over: the gestures and Rest are the eye program's,
played in MuJoCo through the gimbal's command map, as the next Mech's eyes Core will play them. Each starts once it is
scrolled into view, and three play at a time: touch a still one to play it. Drag to turn it; the neck's pan and the
jaw play in Stages 2 and 3 in [the viewer](https://yellowpine.com/noggin/viewer/). Pictures are stills alone (#839).
