# Pictures

The Mech (#51) as its model has it: our own parts and our Envelopes of the hardware we own, posed by MuJoCo, drawn by
Blender in CI on every merge to main and never kept in git (ADR 0009, ADR 0024). Blender renders six
stills (the front doors below and a poster per Stage); everything else here is the web viewer, which draws Blender's
GLBs in the same look (ADR 0008). How they are made: [Model notes](model-notes.md).

Stage by Stage, in the Preset the Theme bar at the top picks, the default one ([Themes](face-design.md)) unless you
pick another: Stage 1 the eye block on its test stand with the eyes Core, Stage 2
adds the Torso, the neck and the boards, Stage 3 the jaw, the whole Mech. Each from the side, isometric and with every
part apart, each slid out the way it goes on: a servo or horn along its shaft, a part on a pivot along its axis, a board
off its carrier. Drag one to turn it. Under them, the stills the home shows: Stage 1 from the front, Stage 3 isometric
and Stage 3 from the side, exploded.

Stage 1 in the viewer, once per motion, each playing it over and over: the gestures and Rest are the eye program's,
played in MuJoCo through the gimbal's command map, as the eyes Core plays them. Each starts once it is
scrolled into view, and three play at a time: touch a still one to play it. Drag to turn it; the neck's pan and the
jaw play in Stages 2 and 3 in [the viewer](https://yellowpine.com/noggin/viewer/).
