# Bambu PLA Pure facts (filament id GFA19)

Colours (BambuStudio `filaments_color_codes.json`, fila_id GFA19; not in the per-printer profiles):
- Pure White 17100: #FFFFFF
- Absolute Black 17101: #000000
- Milky Pink 17200: #F7CED7
- Apricot 17300: #FFB673
Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/master/resources/profiles/BBL/filament/filaments_color_codes.json

Density:
- PLA Pure profiles ("Bambu PLA Pure @base", https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/master/resources/profiles/BBL/filament/Bambu%20PLA%20Pure%20%40base.json) set no density and have no filament_colour; they inherit fdm_filament_pla.
- fdm_filament_pla: filament_density 1.24 g/cm3 (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/master/resources/profiles/BBL/filament/fdm_filament_pla.json)
- For comparison, PLA Basic @base sets 1.26 (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/master/resources/profiles/BBL/filament/Bambu%20PLA%20Basic%20%40base.json)
- Not found: a density on the eu.store.bambulab.com PLA Pure page (https://eu.store.bambulab.com/products/pla-pure); no TDS checked.

Mechanical values (added 2026-11-02, #626):
- Bambu's PLA Pure TDS is not on Bambu's CDN; MatterHackers' product page reproduces its table and links the TDS
  (https://www.matterhackers.com/store/l/bambu-lab-pla-pure-175mm-1kg/sk/M0K9QEW8). Specimens: Milky Pink on an H2D,
  0.4 mm nozzle, 220 °C, 100% infill, dried 8 h at 50 °C.
- Density 1.24 g/cm3 (ISO 1183), matching fdm_filament_pla above.
- ISO 527, X-Y / Z: Young's modulus 2481 ± 20 / 2196 ± 25 MPa; tensile strength 51.5 ± 2 / 30.0 ± 3 MPa; elongation
  5.6 / 2.8%. ISO 178 bending strength 75 / 55 MPa.
- So across layers PLA Pure is weaker than PLA Basic (35 MPa Z, `2026-11-02-literature-cams-am.md`).
