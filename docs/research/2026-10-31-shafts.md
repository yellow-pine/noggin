# Servo shafts, horns and the remaining hardware values (2026-10-31, #62)

Question: where every servo shaft sits (which tab hole it is near, which face of the printed lug the flange rests on,
which way it points), the horn geometry, and the other assumed hardware values, from the reference files instead of
the calliper. Ground-truth order (CLAUDE.md): Bambu STEP > PurpleShark's STLs > guide PDF images > remote configs >
Cansin's photos > datasheets. Every conflict below names both sources and the winner.

Sources (local copies under docs/references, gitignored):
- STEP-SERVO / STEP-HALF / STEP-LINE / STEP-CROSS: `cyberbrick-cad/servo/CAD_9g Servo Motor with Clutch
  Protection-PG002-PG001/*.stp`, read exactly with OCP (`uv run --with cadquery-ocp`, the reader of
  model/engine/step_mesh.py): cylindrical faces (radius, axis, extent) and solid boxes.
- STL-*: `01-eyes/models`, `02-pan-tilt/models`, `03-mouth/models` (assembled frames, model/geometry.py), read with
  trimesh: ray casts along the hole axes, voxel slices, `model/fits.py` circle fits.
- EYES-pN / PT-pN / MOUTH-pN: the three guide PDFs, page N ("Page N of ..."); pictures extracted with `pdfimages`.
  PT-p7 and MOUTH-p14 are the designer's CAD renders (servos and horns drawn).
- CONFIG: `03-mouth/remote-config-eyes-pandt-jaw.json` (every servo `initial_value` 90).
- PHOTO: docs/build-photos/eyes-lid-linkage-side.jpg (Cansin's build).
- C3: Espressif ESP32-C3 datasheet, Table 5-7 "Wi-Fi Current Consumption Depending on RF Modes"
  (https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32-c3_datasheet_en.pdf).

Method (deterministic, script in the session scratchpad, not committed): for each servo, 2 near holes x 2 shaft
directions x 2 lug faces = 8 placements of STEP-SERVO on the measured hole axes; each is voxelised (0.5 mm, eroded one
voxel so face contacts do not count) against the printed parts around it; survivors are then decided by the guide
pictures and by the linkage closing at the designer's neutral (CONFIG: 90 for all), and the result is checked against
the measured faces the bars must ride on.

## Servo STEP (STEP-SERVO), local mm

- Tab plate z -2.3..0 (2.3 thick); tab holes r 1.1 at x -2.55 and 25.25, y 5.9, counterbore r 2.1 z -1.3..0. Case
  x -0.1..22.8, y -0.1..11.9; body z 0..16.3 on the +z side of the tabs; gear housing to z -10.0 (boss r 5.3), collar
  r 2.4..3.1 to z -11.2, output spline r 2.35 from z -11.2 to the tip at z -14.52 (3.32 long), screw hole r 0.95.
- Shaft at x 16.69: 8.56 from the near hole (25.25), 19.24 from the far one.
- Lead: three wire solids x 16.69..41.99 at z 10.55..11.47 leave the +x end face (x 22.8), i.e. **the cable leaves
  the end nearest the shaft**, 5 mm above the body bottom.

## Horn STEPs

- All three horns: hub r 3.63, 4.15 tall (local z 0..4.15); spline bore r 2.15 from the z 4.15 face down to z 1.8
  (2.35 deep); screw hole r 1.25 (z 1.1..1.9); screw-head recess r 2.45, 1.1 deep in the z 0 face; arm 1.6 thick on
  z 0..1.6. So **z 4.15 is the servo side and the arm lies on the outer face** (z 0).
- Arm holes r 0.5 every 2 mm: half arm (STEP-HALF) at 4.75, 6.75, 8.75, 10.75 (r 0.55), 12.75, 14.75, tip r 1.9 at
  14.15; line arm (STEP-LINE) the same both sides; cross arm (STEP-CROSS) long side 4.75..16.75, short sides 4.75 and
  6.75. "Second from last" (EYES-p15, PT-p11) on the half arm = 12.75 (geometry.HORN_STEP pin, unchanged).
- Seat: the horn goes on until the spline tip (z -14.52) meets the bore bottom: inner face at STEP z -12.17,
  outer face at -16.32, i.e. 11.52 beyond geometry's tab plane (z -0.65), outer face 15.67.
  Conflict: geometry.SERVO_STEP horn_seat 10.35 (Fusion measure of the collar top, z -11.0) puts the hub 1.17 mm down
  the spline into the bore bottom; STEP wins: horn_seat 11.52. Three independent checks agree with 11.52: the eyeball
  bar (STL y 2.5) lands on the eye horn's outer face at y 2.52; the tilt bar between the horn's outer face (x 10.98) and
  the tilt arm lever face (x 8.0) is 2.98 for a 3.0 bar; the lid horn's outer face (x -23.74) is 0.26 from the lid
  ears' outer face (x -24.0).
  Conflict: the model placed the horn with local +z away from the servo (arm against the servo, hub outward); the
  bore/recess says the opposite. STEP wins: the horn is turned over (same extents along the shaft, arm outermost).

## Per servo (frames as model/geometry.py; "lug" = the printed tab plate with the two screw holes)

### Lid servos (L; R mirrors about x 29.5)
- Lug x -7.42..-3.40 (STL-L servo bracket; the face is -7.34 at the upper hole, 0.08 proud at the lower one).
- Voxel test: only placements with the tab on the lug's outer face (x < -7.42) clear the bracket; both shaft
  directions and both near holes survive it.
- Shaft points -X (outboard): EYES-p32 photos and PHOTO show the horn and crank bars outside the bracket.
- Near hole = the upper one (y -4.65): in PHOTO and EYES-p32 the horn hub is about 10 mm below the upper tab screw and
  18 below the lower (STEP: 8.56 / 19.24). The four-bar agrees: with the horn along +Y at neutral the pin is at
  (y -0.46, z 40), 41.9 and 41.6 from the two lid pins for 41 mm bars, symmetric about the eye centre; the lower hole
  would give 46.2 and 40.0.
- Flange: tab's body side on the lug's outer face, body through the bracket window, gear housing outboard.
- Result: tab plane x -8.07; horn seat x -19.59, outer face -23.74; shaft (y -13.21, z 40.0). Was (-16.0, -13.5, 40.0)
  inferred. Settled.
- The straight eyelid bar rides on the lid ears' outer face (x -24..-27, [M]) 0.26 off the horn face; LID_LINKS
  unchanged (the old "4.1 mm off the horn face" note is gone).

## Other values

- Core radio: C3 Table 5-7 (chip, 3.3 V, peak): TX 335 mA at 802.11b 1 Mbps 21 dBm, RX 84 mA (802.11b/g/n HT20).
  The model's WIFI_TX_A / WIFI_RX_A already use these; now cited instead of "(assumed)". The Core's own regulator
  path is not published.
- XH2.54 lead 100 mm: PT-p8 hardware list "100mm Wire with 2Pin XH2.54 Connectors" (EYES-p11 prints "00mm", a typo).
- Servo lead length: not in STEP-SERVO (the wires are cut 25 mm past the shaft), not on the store page
  (https://eu.store.bambulab.com/en-si/products/9g-servo-motor-with-clutch-protection) or in the electrical note. Stays
  assumed 250 mm: calliper/ruler.
- Mouth channel: CONFIG has the mouth on receiver 2 PWM2; MOUTH-p18 text says "the third position". Config is the
  higher-ranked machine source here only by being the file actually loaded; geometry.CHANNELS (PWM2) unchanged.
