# Servo shafts, horns and the remaining hardware values (2026-10-31, #62)

Question: where every servo shaft sits (which tab hole it is near, which face of the printed lug the flange rests on,
which way it points), the horn geometry, and the other assumed hardware values, from the reference files. Ground-truth order (CLAUDE.md): Bambu STEP > PurpleShark's STLs > guide PDF images > remote configs >
Cansin's photos > datasheets. Every conflict below names both sources and the winner.

Sources (local copies under docs/references, gitignored):
- STEP-SERVO / STEP-HALF / STEP-LINE / STEP-CROSS: `cyberbrick-cad/servo/CAD_9g Servo Motor with Clutch
  Protection-PG002-PG001/*.stp`, read exactly with OCP (`uv run --with cadquery-ocp`, the reader of
  model/engine/step_mesh.py): cylindrical faces (radius, axis, extent) and solid boxes.
- STL-*: `01-eyes/models`, `02-pan-tilt/models`, `03-mouth/models` (assembled frames, model/geometry.py), read with
  trimesh: ray casts along the hole axes, voxel slices, `model/fits.py` circle fits.
- EYES-pN / PT-pN / MOUTH-pN: the three guide PDFs, page N ("Page N of ..."); pictures extracted with `pdfimages`.
  PT-p7 and MOUTH-p14 are the designer's CAD renders (servos and horns drawn).
- CONFIG: `03-mouth/remote-config-eyes-pandt-jaw.json` (every servo `initial_value` 90).
- PHOTO: docs/references/build-photos/eyes-lid-linkage-side.jpg (Cansin's build).
- C3: Espressif ESP32-C3 datasheet, Table 5-7 "Wi-Fi Current Consumption Depending on RF Modes"
  (https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32-c3_datasheet_en.pdf).

Method (deterministic, script in the session scratchpad, not committed): for each servo, 2 near holes x 2 shaft
directions x 2 lug faces = 8 placements of STEP-SERVO on the measured hole axes; each is voxelised (0.5 mm, eroded one
voxel so face contacts do not count) against the printed parts around it; survivors are then decided by the guide
pictures and by the linkage closing at the designer's neutral (CONFIG: 90 for all), and the result is checked against
the measured faces the bars must ride on.

## Mating check (#67, model/engine/mating.py, `make sim`)

Every screw, pin and shaft joint is checked on the meshes: each part's round feature (hole, pin, spline, bore) fitted
near its declared centre (fits.py), carried by the part's placement; axes within 0.2 mm. Each servo must touch the mount
its near tab screw goes into and enter no part (the horn on the simplified spline excepted), and its lead must leave
the case end through no printed part. 64 joints over the three sub-assemblies, plus the seven seats and leads (tab screws, splines, horn pins, bar
pins, lid ears on the bracket pins, eyeball screws and pivots, bracket feet, jaw hinges, mount feet, the tilt and pan
pivots, the upright screw); the electronics are not in it (their STEP holes are not clean circles; their standoff
placement is stages.placements). First run on the #62 model, and what settled each:

- Lid bars on the lid pins: 0.63 (outside) and 0.90 (inside) mm. Conflict: the lid STLs' pose with the 41 mm bars
  (STL-eyelid bar/crank, fits 41.0) vs the horn along +Y at neutral (an inference of the model, no source). STLs win:
  the horn's neutral arm turns 3.59 deg from +Y toward the lids (geometry.LID_HORN_ARM), the best fit of both loops:
  the pin is 41.14 from the inside lid's pin and 40.86 from the outside one's, 0.14 mm left at each (no one horn angle
  closes both: the STL lids are 0.28 apart in that sense). The lid curves move: +20 deg horn now gives inside 21.02 /
  outside -19.81 deg (was 21.24 / -19.33). The bars' placement tolerance drops from inferred (3 mm) to measured.
- Eye servos 0.10 mm into the L/R servo bracket. Conflict: the eye lug holes (STL, fitted z 26.88) put the case side
  (STEP-SERVO case y -0.1..11.9: 6.0 from the hole axis) at z 20.9, under the bracket floor face z 21.0 (STL). Both
  STLs stand: the case rests on the floor, shaft z 27.0, and the tab screws take 0.12 (inside the 0.2). The eyeball
  bar's horn hole is at z 27.0 too (STL), so the eye horn pin is now purely radial: 12.56 from the shaft against the
  STEP horn's 12.75 hole, 0.19 mm (passes; the bar self-taps its r 1.05 hole).
- Tilt servo: far tab screw 0.22 mm, gear housing 0.11 into the far tower block. Conflict: the tower's holes are 27.58
  apart (STL-PandT pan base, fitted y -5.04 / 22.54), the servo's tabs 27.8 (STEP-SERVO). Each part keeps its own;
  the servo sits centred on the pair (each screw 0.11 off) and its housing end then clears the block face (y -2.58).
  Shaft y 13.98 -> 14.09.
- Pan servo lead into the plain mount. Conflict (above): the STL has the cutout mount at the far end, the lead leaves
  at the shaft end (STEP-SERVO) through the cutout (PT-p16 text and photo). STEP + guide win over the STL's placement:
