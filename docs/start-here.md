# Start here

noggin is a Mech on your desk that reacts to Claude Code: a head whose eyes, lids, neck and jaw move as a session
starts, thinks, answers and waits on you.

## Where it stands

Nothing is printed yet: the Mech is designed in its model, and every size in it is inferred until it is printed and
built (#51). The guides read the model as it is today, so you can follow the design and turn each Stage in
[the viewer](https://yellowpine.com/noggin/viewer/), but don't buy parts for a Stage until its guide says it is built.

## The order of work

1. **Parts**: what the Mech is made of and what to buy ([Parts](build/parts.md)).
2. **Stage 1, the eyes**: the eye block on its test stand with the eyes Core ([Stage 1](build/stage-1.md)).
3. **Stage 2, the neck**: the head on the neck over the Torso, the neck Core beside the eyes Core
   ([Stage 2](build/stage-2.md)).
4. **Stage 3, the mouth**: the jaw, the whole Mech ([Stage 3](build/stage-3.md)).
5. **Wire, flash and calibrate** each Core ([Wiring](build/wiring.md), [Flashing](build/flashing.md),
   [Calibration](build/calibration.md)).
6. **Run it**: the noggin service on your Mac and the Claude Code hooks ([Use](use/noggin-service.md)).
